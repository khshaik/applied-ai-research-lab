# S8 foundational comparison query control

Status: **C07 complete — accepted developmental controls; not a systematic corpus**  
Cutoff used for query development: **16 August 2026**

## Scope and boundary

S8 covers five comparison functions: Story Point and relative-effort validity,
developer-productivity measurement, human modern-code-review work,
retrospective mental-workload measurement, and software delivery flow. Records
used only as methods, instruments, or foundational theory remain in a separate
method/reference stratum; they are not GenAI primary studies.

The strict metadata-level query-appraisal rule required substantive analysis of
at least one of those functions. Generic tool/system performance, technical
automation, or a motivational mention of productivity was insufficient.

## Development history

- v0.1 was rejected as overbroad after capped totals of 11,162 OpenAlex and
  8,415 Semantic Scholar records.
- v0.2 narrowed the concepts; Semantic Scholar completed, but OpenAlex failed
  closed on duplicate cursor identifiers.
- v0.3 narrowed further, but OpenAlex again failed closed under relevance-order
  pagination.
- v0.4 added deterministic OpenAlex publication-date sorting and completed, but
  missed three positive sentinels because of plural/title-token variants.
- v0.5 repaired those variants, but a generic queuing-formula clause raised the
  OpenAlex burden above 1,000 and was rejected before appraisal.
- v0.6 replaced that broad clause with software-Kanban evidence. Little's
  original queueing identity remains a separately verified method/reference
  anchor to be retrieved through DOI verification and citation chasing.

## Interpretation

C07 demonstrates that the S8 translations can retrieve a balanced and
operationally reviewable foundational comparison set in both mandatory open
indexes. It does not validate VDCM, establish empirical superiority over Story
Points, prove a cognitive-load construct, or support an exhaustive-literature
claim. Those conclusions remain dependent on the frozen systematic evidence
map, lawful full-text appraisal, study-family consolidation, and Route A future
organizational validation.
