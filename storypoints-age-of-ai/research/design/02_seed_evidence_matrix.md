## Seed-set implications

1. “Human attention replaces Story Points” is not a sufficient contribution.
2. The HIE papers must anchor and constrain the theoretical model rather than be treated as peripheral related work.
3. HADR constructs should be derived after review; the six Gate 1 dimensions are hypotheses, not confirmed factors.
4. The empirical unit should move from developer task effort to **verified delivery capacity across roles and gates**.
5. A paper without prospective multi-team data, Story Point comparison, and delivery-quality outcomes is likely too incremental.
6. Readiness gates alone are not novel; their contribution would be their coupling to attention forecasting and outcome validation.
