# Research ethics and responsible-use statement — working version

## Current Route B study

The current study consists of an open-index evidence map, design-science
artifact, and developmental synthetic simulation. It does not recruit or
interact with human participants, intervene in an organization, use patient or
employee records, or process identifiable personal data as research data.
Consequently, no human-participant ethics approval is claimed or implied. The
accountable author and institution must determine whether the conference or
local policy requires a formal exemption or not-applicable declaration.

Bibliographic metadata are obtained from public scholarly services. Full texts
are accessed lawfully; copyrighted reports are not redistributed merely because
they were reviewed. Extracted support snippets remain limited and source
locators are retained for verification.

## Responsible use of the proposed artifact

The role-constrained verified delivery artifact is intended for work-item and
role-pool planning. It must not be used for:

- individual employee surveillance, ranking, compensation, or disciplinary
  decisions;
- inferring psychological workload or cognitive ability from service time;
- automatically approving security, compliance, release, or business
  acceptance;
- suppressing professional judgment or treating an illustrative estimate as a
  performance target; or
- concealing unresolved evidence obligations through aggregation.

Any later Route A study requires a separate protocol addressing informed
consent or lawful organizational basis, data minimization, work-log privacy,
access control, retention, minimum reporting cell sizes, gaming, measurement
burden, disparate effects, and the right to challenge automated recommendations.
