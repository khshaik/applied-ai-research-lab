# Manuscript Scientific Freeze

**Freeze date:** 2026-08-19  
**Scope:** scientific positioning before venue-specific formatting

## Title

**Beyond Story Points in AI-Assisted Delivery: An Evidence Map and Simulation
Framework for Role-Constrained Human Capacity**

## Paper type

Framework-development and design-science paper combining a targeted,
access-constrained open evidence map with developmental synthetic simulation.
It is not an organizational validation or human-subject cognitive-load study.

## Contribution statement

The contribution is a prospective role-stage resource-and-flow representation
that couples pre-commitment demand drivers, active human service demand,
effective role capacity, explicit queues, evidence readiness, dependencies,
bounded rework, and verified-completion forecasts. It extends rather than
replaces prior Story Point, HIE, agentic-cost, gate, and delivery-orchestration
work.

## Research questions

1. How does accessible evidence characterize human-work redistribution,
   review, assurance, coordination, and estimation in AI-assisted delivery?
2. Which pre-commitment constructs can represent role-constrained,
   evidence-ready delivery without conflating time, capacity, queueing, and
   psychological workload?
3. Under which declared synthetic conditions do explicit role-stage demand,
   queues, dependencies, readiness, and rework alter verified-completion
   forecasts relative to simpler comparators?
4. When does the proposed detail add no material value, become unstable, or
   impose unjustified estimation overhead?

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No universal Story Point failure or replacement claim.
- No exhaustive-literature or absolute novelty claim.
- No validated human attention, cognition, workload, organizational
  calibration, causal impact, ROI, or universal-superiority claim.
- All simulation findings remain developmental and synthetic.
- All evidence-map claims retain the open-source cutoff and D14 resource cap.

Any material broadening requires a new accountable-author confirmation record.
