# Author Metadata Required for Identified Package

Complete this record before G03/G06. Do not place it in the anonymous package.

## Authors in publication order

For each author provide:

- full publication name;
- affiliation name and department;
- city, state/region, postal code, and country;
- institutional email;
- ORCID, if available;
- corresponding-author status; and
- contribution roles.

## Declarations

- competing interests;
- funding source and grant number, or explicit no-funding statement;
- acknowledgements;
- author-contribution statement;
- corresponding-author mailing/contact details required by the venue; and
- permission confirmation from every co-author for the final order and text.

## Integrity rule

Author metadata will not be inferred from repository paths, workbook ownership,
email accounts, or prior projects. The accountable author must supply and
approve it explicitly.
