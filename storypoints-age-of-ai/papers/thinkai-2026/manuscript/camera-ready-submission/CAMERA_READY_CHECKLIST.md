# THINKAI 2026 Camera-Ready Checklist

Complete this checklist only after acceptance and after receiving final
organizer instructions.

## Acceptance and reviewer revision

- [ ] Archive the acceptance notification and authoritative camera-ready instructions.
- [ ] Record every reviewer/editor comment and its disposition.
- [ ] Apply only evidence-supported revisions.
- [ ] Preserve the Route B boundary: synthetic mechanism evidence is not empirical organizational validation.
- [ ] Preserve the bounded evidence-map and novelty wording approved at D17.
- [ ] Re-run every calculation, test, figure, and claim check affected by a substantive revision.
- [ ] Update exact evidence locators if wording or citations change.
- [ ] Reverify citation metadata and DOI bindings.

## Identity and declarations

- [ ] Obtain accountable-author confirmation of author names and order.
- [ ] Confirm affiliations, postal details, corresponding-author email, and ORCID identifiers.
- [ ] Confirm author contributions and acknowledgements.
- [ ] Confirm funding and competing-interest statements.
- [ ] Confirm the applicable ethics or not-applicable statement.
- [ ] Retain an accurate generative-AI assistance disclosure.
- [ ] Confirm data/code availability, repository URL, licence, and archival DOI when available.
- [ ] Ensure no author metadata is inferred from local paths, accounts, or prior projects.

## Production package

- [ ] Confirm the final THINKAI/Springer source template and page limit.
- [ ] Confirm required source, figure, supplementary-material, and declaration formats.
- [ ] Generate a newly versioned identified DOCX and PDF; do not overwrite the v0.2 baseline.
- [ ] Inspect every rendered page for clipping, overlap, pagination, table, equation, reference, and figure defects.
- [ ] Verify alt text, captions, figure resolution, colour mode, and accessibility requirements.
- [ ] Check visible content, filenames, and document/PDF metadata.
- [ ] Run the manuscript, citation, DOI, language, duplication-risk, and public-release checks.
- [ ] Create a checksum manifest for the exact approved submission files.
- [ ] Obtain G06 scientific, citation, authorship, and release approval.
- [ ] Obtain G07 submission authorization and archive the submission receipt.

## Repository and artifact release

- [ ] Follow the conference's anonymity and supplementary-material policy.
- [ ] Do not redistribute copyrighted full texts.
- [ ] Exclude credentials, local environments, caches, temporary files, and restricted records.
- [ ] Confirm that published artifacts match their release manifest and contain no secret findings.
