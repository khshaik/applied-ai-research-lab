### 2.1 Prior-year submission precedent—not a 2026 rule

The official THINKAI 2025 submission page specifies 12–15 pages in the
LNCS/CCIS one-column format and double-blind review. The official THINKAI 2024
page states the same requirements. This consistent precedent is useful for
working layout planning, but it does not establish the 2026 limit or review
policy. Until the 2026 organizer publishes or confirms them, G01 remains open
and the manuscript must retain both anonymous and identified variants.

- [THINKAI 2025 paper submission](https://thinkai.klh.edu.in/paper-submission)

## 3. Springer proceedings preparation controls

Springer provides computer-science proceedings author instructions and Word/
LaTeX templates. Springer explicitly states that the maximum pages per paper
are set by the conference, so a page limit must not be inferred from the generic
template.

- [Springer conference-proceedings author resources](https://www.springernature.com/in/authors/publish-a-book/step-by-step-conference-proceedings)
- [Computer-science proceedings author instructions](https://dam.springernature.com/file/7OxknSGeaYnBZScg7ZTMkm/%2A/Springer_Instructions_for_Authors_of_Proceedings_CS-1.pdf?authcred=Q29weVVSTDpDMHB5X1VSTA%3D%3D)

Current proceedings instructions require textual alternatives for non-text
content. Every manuscript figure and image-format table therefore needs concise
alt text in the source package.

## 4. AI-use controls

Springer Nature's current guidance requires:

- accountable humans for accuracy, originality, integrity, and final text;
- no AI system listed as an author;
- transparent declaration when generative AI assisted text, analysis, or other
  substantive content;
- verification and referencing of AI-assisted output;
- protection of confidential, personal, sensitive, and copyrighted material;
  and
- caution concerning generative-AI images.

Source: [Springer Nature AI guidance](https://group.springernature.com/gp/group/ai/ai-guidance-for-our-researchers-and-communities).

The manuscript's framework and method figures are deterministic vector diagrams
generated from checked-in plotting code and author-defined scientific content;
the result figure is generated from checksummed synthetic data. They are not
photorealistic image-generation outputs. Because AI assisted their design and
code, that assistance will nevertheless be declared, and the accountable author
must review every figure and alt-text description.

## 5. Confirmed preparation decisions

- Maintain both anonymous-review and identified-author variants until the
  conference states which is required.
- Use 12–15 Springer one-column pages and double-blind review only as a
  provisional working-layout assumption based on 2024–2025 precedent, never as
  a confirmed 2026 requirement.
- Use the current Springer computer-science proceedings structure as the
  working template, but do not enforce a final page limit yet.
- Add alt text to all six working figures.
- Include a substantive AI-assistance disclosure; do not treat the use as copy
  editing only.
- Keep citation and claim confirmation under accountable-human control.
- Do not upload a confidential submitted manuscript into unapproved review
  tools after submission.

  imagery.

  identified packages. G01 remains partial until a 2026 organizer source
