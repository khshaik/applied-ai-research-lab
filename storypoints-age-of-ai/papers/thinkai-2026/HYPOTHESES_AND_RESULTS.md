# Research Questions, Propositions, and Result Boundaries

The paper is a framework-development and developmental-simulation study, not a
confirmatory organizational trial. P1–P7 therefore remain future empirical
propositions rather than tested human-subject hypotheses.

## Evidence-map result

The open evidence map included 791 study families and found no family that met
all five predeclared overlap dimensions for the same pre-commitment planning
use. The permitted conclusion is bounded by the cutoff, open-source coverage,
and approved D14 resource cap.

## Simulation result

Developmental scenarios demonstrate that role constraints, readiness,
dependencies, and queues can alter conditional forecast behavior. They do not
establish real-world superiority. Performance varies across worlds, and simpler
comparators outperform the proposed model in some scenarios.

## Future empirical tests

Prospective Route A work must evaluate incremental prediction, calibration,
bottleneck forecasting, readiness-risk association, workload redistribution,
quality-aware capacity, and transportability with genuine practitioners and
organizational event data.
