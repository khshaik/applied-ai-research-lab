# Acceptance Risk Assessment

## Principal scientific risks

- **Coverage:** subscription databases were inaccessible; the evidence-map
  conclusion is bounded to the predeclared open sources and citation network.
- **Screening:** isolated AI-agent passes are not human inter-rater reliability.
- **Construct validity:** Route B does not validate psychological workload,
  cognition, organizational calibration, causal impact, or ROI.
- **Novelty:** no duplicate was identified within the bounded search; this is
  not proof that no related work exists.
- **Simulation:** comparator performance varies, and simpler comparators win in
  some developmental worlds.
- **Venue:** current page-limit and anonymization rules require final
  authoritative confirmation before release.

## Controls

- Preserve the exact D17 wording and locators.
- Report evidence strata and quality bands; do not pool them as equivalent.
- Keep touch time separate from queue delay and psychological workload.
- Label all simulation results developmental and synthetic.
- Maintain separate anonymous and identified packages.
- Fail release if claims, references, declarations, hashes, or visual QA remain
  unresolved.
