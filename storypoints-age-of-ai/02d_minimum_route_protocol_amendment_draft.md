# Minimum-Route Protocol Amendment — Draft for Accountable-Author Approval

**Amendment version:** 0.1

**Applies to:** `02_systematic_review_protocol.md`, version 1.2  
**Status:** `approved_effective`

## 1. Decision requested

Approve a minimum defensible THINKAI 2026 paper route comprising:

1. a targeted, AI-assisted evidence map using declared open-index/source-family pairs;
2. a design-science specification of the Verified Delivery Capacity Model (VDCM) and Role–Stage Demand and Readiness Instrument (RSDRI);
3. reproducible developmental simulation and mechanism-ablation evidence; and
4. an explicit future Route A for human and organizational validation.

This route is narrower than a comprehensive systematic review and materially narrower than the prospective multi-team empirical study described in protocol version 1.2.

## 2. Reason for the amendment

Protocol version 1.2 is not frozen. It currently requires every search family to be translated and run in OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, and arXiv. That Cartesian requirement is neither methodologically necessary nor source-appropriate:

- the indexes differ in field semantics, disciplinary coverage, pagination, and citation-network capability;
- Crossref is DOI/deposit metadata infrastructure, not a disciplinary discovery index capable of supporting absence claims;
- six subscription sources are lawfully inaccessible without institutional authorization;
- broad developmental queries already show substantial overlap and noise across families;
- the intended contribution is framework development and conditional simulation behavior, not an exhaustive prevalence estimate or universal novelty proof.

The amendment therefore allocates each evidence family to sources suited to its role, retains citation chasing, and narrows all completeness and novelty claims accordingly.

## 3. Frozen paper route after approval

### 3.1 Provisional title

> **Beyond Story Points in AI-Assisted Delivery: An Evidence Map and Simulation Framework for Role-Constrained Human Capacity**

### 3.2 Paper type

Design-science framework paper supported by a targeted open evidence map and developmental discrete-event simulation scenarios.

### 3.3 Research contribution

The proposed contribution is a prospective resource-and-flow representation that:

- forecasts role-by-lifecycle-stage human touch demand at commitment time;
- separates active touch time from constrained-role queue delay;
- represents evidence readiness, role capacity, dependencies, and gate transitions; and
- estimates verified delivery capacity under explicit scenario assumptions.

It is an extension and integration of prior estimation, human-oversight, lifecycle, and delivery-flow work—not the first AI-era effort model, human-gate workflow, or lifecycle framework.

### 5.1 Crossref boundary

Crossref is used to verify DOI, title, author, venue, date, type, and version metadata for candidate or included records. It is not a mandatory broad discovery source and its non-retrieval cannot support an absence or novelty claim.

### 5.2 Subscription-source boundary

Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, and ScienceDirect remain recorded as inaccessible on 15 August 2026 because authorized institutional access is unavailable. They are coverage limitations, not silently substituted sources. No access control will be bypassed.

### 5.3 Practitioner evidence

Practitioner and official-framework sources remain a separately labelled contextual stratum. They may support terminology and implementation context but cannot be counted or weighted as peer-reviewed empirical evidence.

## 6. Search and review controls retained unchanged

Approval of this amendment does not relax the following controls:

- literal, source-accepted, versioned queries for every declared pair;
- positive and neutral/disconfirming sentinel tests;
- complete pagination, raw-response archiving, timestamps, and SHA-256 manifests;
- deterministic query-precision appraisal and acceptance rules;
- a new post-freeze systematic corpus, separate from developmental exports;
- DOI/arXiv/title/author/year deduplication and study-family consolidation;
- two isolated agent screening passes on an identical checksummed packet;
- separate agent adjudication with agent concordance reported instead of human inter-rater reliability;
- lawful full-text retrieval, design-appropriate appraisal, exact extraction locators, and bidirectional citation chasing;
- accountable-author confirmation of every material cited claim before submission; and
- distinct peer-reviewed, preprint, secondary, practitioner, and method/reference strata.

## 7. Revised freeze criteria

After this amendment is approved, protocol version 1.3 may be frozen only when:

1. every declared source-family pair in Section 5 has an accepted literal query and complete control record;
2. Crossref is implemented only as the verification role in Section 5.1;
3. inaccessible-source records, citation-chasing rules, screening prompts, criteria, evidence strata, appraisal, extraction, and author-confirmation controls are reconciled;
4. the evidence-map reporting boundary and claims in Section 8 are embedded in the manuscript prompt and templates;
5. all protocol, query, schema, prompt, control, and approval artifacts are versioned and checksummed; and
6. the accountable author separately approves the final freeze package.

Developmental searches and counts cannot enter the systematic corpus. Accepted queries must be rerun after freeze into a new corpus.

## 8. Maximum permitted claims

Subject to successful evidence-map execution and manuscript verification, the paper may claim only:

- VDCM/RSDRI is a design-science framework and operational specification;
- the review is a targeted, access-constrained, AI-assisted open evidence map;
- the simulation demonstrates conditional behavior in declared synthetic scenarios;
- the implementation is a verified developmental prototype with reproducible manifests; and
- no substantively duplicative framework was identified within the predeclared open indexes, repositories, and citation networks searched through the stated cutoff date, if the final novelty adjudication supports that statement.

The paper must not claim:

- exhaustive literature coverage or that no prior research exists;
- validated measurement of human attention or cognitive load;
- empirical superiority over Story Points, HIE, or organizational planning practice;
- real-world calibration, transportability, usability, or return on investment;
- a causal effect of readiness gates, AI use, or the proposed planning framework;
- completed prospective multi-team validation; or
- a locked production simulation evaluation unless the project route is explicitly upgraded and all pre-lock controls pass.

Negative or mixed comparator results must be reported and cannot be reframed as universal superiority.

## 9. Explicit exclusions from the minimum completion route

The following are future work unless separately approved as a scope upgrade:

- prospective multi-team Route A data collection;
- human construct, content, cognitive-load, or usability validation;
- subscription-database access;
- production locked-simulation execution and its release/seal controls; and
- organizational ROI or causal intervention evaluation.

## 10. Effect on protocol version 1.2

On approval, protocol version 1.3 must:

1. replace the Cartesian source-family requirement in Sections 9 and 19.1 with Section 5 of this amendment;
2. replace the prospective multi-team novelty hypothesis and working title with Sections 3 and 8;
3. identify Crossref as metadata verification rather than a broad discovery source;
4. preserve all screening, appraisal, extraction, traceability, and accountable-author controls; and
5. preserve the bounded novelty wording and inaccessible-source limitation.

Until those edits are reconciled, validated, checksummed, and approved, the protocol remains `draft_unfrozen`.
