# Gate 2 Grey-Literature Search Procedure

## Purpose and separation

This procedure identifies operational constructs, measurement practices, and organizational evidence. Grey records remain Stream B and must not be pooled with scholarly studies as equivalent evidence.

## Reproducible source set

Search these official domains separately: `dora.dev`, `github.blog`, `github.com/github`, `microsoft.com/research`, `research.google`, `about.gitlab.com`, `nasa.gov`, `nist.gov`, `iso.org`, and `sahaj.ai`. Add another domain only through a deviation record stating why it is authoritative and relevant.

For each domain, run the following short families with the search engine's exact-phrase behavior documented:

```text
site:DOMAIN ("generative AI" OR "AI-assisted") (software development OR software delivery) (productivity OR quality OR flow)
site:DOMAIN (Copilot OR "coding assistant") (review OR testing OR security OR verification) (effort OR workload OR time)
site:DOMAIN ("AI-assisted development" OR "coding agent") (readiness OR governance OR approval OR measurement)
```

Also use the official site's native search, if available, with the equivalent short terms. Inspect the first 100 web results per family or all results when fewer are exposed, plus a date-sorted 2025–2026 pass where supported. Log the engine, exact query, date, depth, and URLs inspected; never report an approximate engine count as the corpus size.

## Inclusion and appraisal

A grey record must add a distinct operational construct or traceable observation and provide an identifiable owner, date/version, method or evidence trail, and stable URL/document. Vendor outcome claims without inspectable methods are contextual only. Score included records with the protocol's AACODS-inspired Authority, Accuracy, Coverage, Objectivity, Date, and Significance form; record sponsorship, product interest, sample, observed versus modeled data, and raw-material availability.

## Versioning and duplication

Download or archive the permitted document, record its SHA-256, and capture publication/update date. Consolidate reports, blog summaries, and peer-reviewed papers into one study family. Prefer the primary report; retain derivative pages only when they contain unique operational detail.

## Stopping and update rule

Stop a domain-family search after the declared depth, not when results appear repetitive. Snowball references from included official reports. Re-run the same families within seven days of submission and record new/changed records. Search-engine personalization and ranking instability are limitations to report.

## Agent-review disclosure

Two isolated agent passes may perform eligibility and AACODS screening. The coordinating agent verifies the source text and adjudicates conflicts. This is agent-assisted evidence mapping, not human double screening. An accountable author must verify included sources and claims before submission.
