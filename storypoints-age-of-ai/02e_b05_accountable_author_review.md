developmental simulation behavior. It may not claim exhaustive retrieval,
validated human cognition, real-world calibration, causal intervention effects,
organizational ROI, universal superiority, completed Route A validation, or a
locked production evaluation.

The six inaccessible subscription sources remain coverage limitations. Crossref
is DOI/bibliographic verification only and cannot support an absence claim.
