# Gate 3B Naming Collision Audit

**Search date:** 13 August 2026  
**Status:** Preliminary web collision screen; not a trademark/legal clearance

## Findings

- The exact phrase **Verified Delivery Capacity Model** did not produce a direct software-engineering framework match in the preliminary searches.
- `VDCM` is already used prominently for virtual digital-content-management products/infrastructure, creating acronym ambiguity.
- The exact phrase **Role–Stage Demand and Readiness Instrument** did not produce a direct match.
- `RSDRI` is already used for Remote Sensing Drought Risk Index and statistical relative-standard-deviation terminology, creating avoidable cross-domain ambiguity.
- Generic phrases combining delivery capacity, readiness, role-based capacity, scenario modeling, and release evidence are already common in commercial planning/delivery products. Novelty cannot rest on terminology.

## Recommendation

Use descriptive full terms during method development and avoid branding claims:

- **framework:** Role-Constrained Verified Delivery Model;
- **planning representation:** Role–Stage Demand Profile;
- **gate representation:** Evidence Readiness State;
- **principal output:** Verified Delivery Capacity.

Do not use VDCM or RSDRI in the paper title. A final acronym should be selected only after the systematic search, scholarly database collision check, domain review, and—if organizational deployment is intended—appropriate legal/trademark review.
