Status: complete

## Method

All 570 D11 inclusions were appraised under protocol §13 using the appropriate
quantitative/mixed, qualitative, secondary-review, conceptual/framework, or
AACODS-inspired form. Each applicable criterion was scored 0–2 with an exact
page locator. No form permitted N/A. Evidence bands control narrative weight;
low quality did not automatically change eligibility.

The initial partitions were independently cross-audited. Only 3/570 matched on
every form, criterion, band, and critical-flaw field. The remaining 567 were
therefore resolved by a separate source-grounded adjudicator; scores were not
averaged or selected by majority vote. This is AI-agent reproducibility and
adjudication, not human appraisal reliability.

## Boundaries

- Technical correctness, security, or test-quality evidence cannot by itself
  validate human attention, cognitive workload, organizational usefulness, or
  causal effects.
- Evidence bands will govern D13 extraction language and D16 sensitivity
  analyses; they are not exclusion decisions.
- Findings must remain separated by observed, self-reported, modeled,
  conceptual, and mixed evidence nature.
- All work used local checksum-bound extracted text only. No network,
  credentials, Git history, package installation, or executable PDF content
  was used.
