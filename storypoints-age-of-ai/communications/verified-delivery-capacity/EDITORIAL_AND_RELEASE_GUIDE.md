# Editorial and Release Guide

## Intended audiences

- engineering and delivery leaders;
- architects, reviewers, security and quality specialists;
- Agile program and portfolio practitioners;
- software-engineering and human–AI researchers.

## Message hierarchy

1. AI-assisted code generation can move the delivery constraint downstream.
2. Planning should expose role-stage service, capacity, queues, dependencies, and evidence readiness.
3. Verified completion is a distribution and a gate outcome—not a renamed effort score.
4. Added modeling detail must demonstrate incremental value over current practice.
5. Real organizational validation remains future work.

## Mandatory wording boundaries

Use:

- “proposed model,” “developmental simulation,” and “bounded open evidence map”;
- “no substantively duplicative framework was identified within the declared open sources and citation network”;
- “Story Points remain a comparator and may remain adequate in stable settings.”

Do not use:

- “Story Points are obsolete” or “stopped working” as a research conclusion;
- “human attention score,” “validated cognitive load,” or “proven productivity gain”;
- “first framework,” “exhaustive review,” or “no prior work exists”;
- causal or organizational-performance claims from the synthetic simulation.

## Visual use

- Preserve the full caption and conceptual-artifact label.
- Use the PNG for ordinary publication and the SVG for editing or print.
- Do not crop the operating-principle band or remove the interpretation boundary.
- Supply the alt text below wherever the publication system supports it.

**Alt text:** A ten-step, three-phase workflow shows how an AI-assisted work item moves from framing and a frozen planning-time snapshot through demand profiling, role-stage service forecasting, effective capacity, queues, dependencies, evidence-readiness gates, and verified delivery forecasting. A feedback loop reconciles outcomes without leaking post-start data into the original forecast. A guardrail band states that the model is not a cognitive-load measure, individual-surveillance tool, or universal Story Point replacement.

## Pre-release checklist

- [ ] Every numerical statement matches the D17-confirmed manuscript.
- [ ] The bounded novelty sentence includes the open-source and resource-cap limitation.
- [ ] Developmental simulation is labeled synthetic and non-causal.
- [ ] The visual is labeled conceptual rather than empirical.
- [ ] No restricted full text, personal data, credentials, or author-identifying review material is included.
- [ ] Double-blind and venue embargo conditions have been checked.
- [ ] Links and accessibility text have been tested in the target channel.
