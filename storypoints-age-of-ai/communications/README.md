# Communications

This directory contains platform-neutral technical explanations derived from the repository's D17-confirmed research artifacts.

## Available package

- [Verified Delivery Capacity](verified-delivery-capacity/README.md): long- and short-form narratives, a ten-step end-to-end workflow, editable and publication-ready visuals, and release guidance.

## Communication controls

- Public-facing claims must remain within the manuscript and claim-verification ledger.
- Conceptual graphics must be labeled as explanatory rather than empirical evidence.
- Analytical graphics must identify their committed source data and interpretation boundary.
- No communication asset may imply validated cognition, organizational superiority, exhaustive literature coverage, or universal replacement of Story Points.
- Double-blind review, venue embargo, copyright, and author-identification constraints apply before release.
