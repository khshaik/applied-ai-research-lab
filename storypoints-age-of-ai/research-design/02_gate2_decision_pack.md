## 1. Gate verdict

The broad proposition—replace Story Points with a new measure of human attention—is not sufficiently novel. Gate 2 may proceed only with the contribution repositioned as an extension and comparative validation of existing work.

The defensible target contribution is:

> Extend task-level Hybrid Intelligence Effort into prospective, team-level verified-delivery capacity forecasting by measuring pre-commitment demand across constrained lifecycle roles, coupling that demand to risk-scaled evidence-readiness states, and testing incremental out-of-sample validity over Story Points and HIE-compatible baselines for human touch time, queue delay, completion probability, and quality outcomes.

## 2. Prior work that constrains the claim

| Predecessor | Existing contribution | Constraint on this paper |
|---|---|---|
| HIE conceptual framework | Context, transformation, iteration, and human oversight as AI-era effort dimensions | Do not claim the first AI-era human-effort model |
| HIE empirical study | Direct Story Point comparison; interaction/oversight variables improve explanatory adequacy | Treat HIE as the nearest baseline and theoretical foundation |
| ACEM | Human/LLM/infrastructure cost, context/revision factors, HITL intensity | Do not present a scalar HITL/cost score as novel |
| Agile V | Requirements-to-verification workflow and human approval gates | Readiness gates alone are not a contribution |
| Human–AI delivery orchestration study | Analysis, planning, implementation, validation, flow, and quality across three programs | End-to-end coverage alone is not novel |

## 3. Six indispensable differentiators

All six must remain in the final design:

1. predictors recorded before work starts, avoiding leakage from eventual prompt and correction counts;
2. capacity demand for product, architecture, security, development, review, QA, operations, and UAT;
3. active human touch time separated from waiting and constrained-role queue time;
4. prospective multi-team or multi-program evaluation;
5. out-of-sample prediction error and calibration, not only in-sample explanatory fit;
6. incremental-validity comparison against actual Story Points and HIE-compatible variables.

## 4. Method route given current human unavailability

AI agents may perform literature screening, extraction, coding, adversarial review, and synthetic stress tests. They cannot replace human participants or practitioner panels in research about human cognition, attention, usability, or organizational delivery.

Therefore Gate 3 must use one of two explicitly separated routes:

- **Route A — empirical extension:** use genuine, de-identified organizational work-item/event data and accountable organizational participants; retain the prospective validation claim.
- **Route B — current feasible route:** submit a systematic evidence map plus design-science artifact and literature-calibrated queueing/simulation study. Describe the instrument as proposed and simulated, not validated for human cognitive demand.

Until Route A data exists, Route B is the approved planning assumption.

## 5. Claims prohibited at this stage

Do not claim:

- Story Points stopped working;
- the first replacement for Story Points;
- the first human-attention or LLM-aware effort model;
- the first end-to-end AI delivery framework;
- empirical validation of human cognitive load from agent-generated data.

Use the measured claim: Story Points may retain partial task-level explanatory value while omitting AI-interaction, verification, cross-role, queueing, and readiness demands relevant to end-to-end delivery.
