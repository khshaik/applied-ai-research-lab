# Research Governance

## Evidence classes

- **Scoping:** supports query design and novelty mapping; never a PRISMA inclusion by itself.
- **Developmental:** exposed query, simulation, or calibration material used for design and diagnosis.
- **Frozen systematic:** created only by rerunning accepted searches after protocol freeze.
- **Locked synthetic evaluation:** inaccessible until every pre-lock hard stop passes.
- **Route A empirical:** genuine practitioner or organizational evidence collected under an approved prospective protocol.
- **Restricted:** material kept outside Git, including participant data and sealed production seeds.

## Immutability

Frozen records are append-only. Corrections receive a new version and a change record. A failed query, gate, negative result, or adverse scenario remains visible and cannot be overwritten by a more favorable rerun.

## AI-assisted review

Two isolated screening agents and a separate adjudicator may support the evidence map. Their agreement is **agent concordance**, not human inter-rater reliability. An accountable author must confirm included studies, extracted claims, and citations before publication.

AI agents cannot substitute for human participants, practitioner panels, or organizational observations when validating workload, cognition, usability, or delivery effectiveness.

## Search coverage

The approved executable sources are OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar Academic Graph, Crossref REST API, and arXiv. Subscription databases remain access-constrained and must be reported as coverage limitations. Open indexes are non-equivalent supplements.

## Simulation boundary

Route B may demonstrate formal coherence and conditional behavior under declared assumptions. It cannot establish real human attention, organizational superiority, causal effects, ROI, or transportability.

## Publication and double-blind review

Keep the repository private until the venue permits deanonymization. Human authors remain responsible for scientific claims, citations, licensing, conflicts of interest, and disclosure of AI assistance.
