# Novelty Decision Memorandum v1.0

**Date:** 12 August 2026  
**Decision:** **NARROW**  
**Evidence base:** 39-source comparison spanning scholarly work, working papers, standards, official practice, patents, and public implementations

## Decision

Do not proceed with the broad claim of a new “enterprise tokenomics,” “closed-loop AI ROI,” or “dynamic hierarchical token-allocation” framework. Those formulations materially overlap existing work.

Proceed with a narrower, empirically testable contribution provisionally titled:

> **From AI Usage to Auditable Outcomes: An Outcome-Evidence Ledger for Prospective AI Resource-Allocation Decisions**

The narrowed research question is:

> Can an outcome-evidence ledger that requires a predefined outcome contract, independently reviewable evidence, a counterfactual baseline, fully loaded AI resource cost, and explicit attribution confidence reduce false ROI and incorrect scale/stop decisions compared with consumption-only, self-reported-value, and conventional cost-quality accounting?

## Why narrowing is mandatory

The closest work already covers most broad components:

- `S01` connects token costs to workflow production, enterprise allocation, value, and marginal productivity.
- `S03` frames agentic systems as risk-adjusted marginal token allocators.
- `S07` supplies expected-ROI AI project and portfolio selection.
- `S18` proposes a closed-loop enterprise AI value and ROI-optimization framework.
- `S19` combines strategic readiness, ROIC value drivers, risk adjustment, stage gates, and decision scorecards.
- `S08` explicitly analyzes carry-over, pooling, transfer, conversion, and trade of AI token usage rights.
- `S10–S12` cover AI FinOps, allocation, unit economics, budgets, optimization, and value alignment.
- `S13–S17` already implement or standardize telemetry, evaluation, cost tracking, budgets, routing, and traces.
- `S34–S37` establish patent boundaries around cost prediction, acceptance-aware routing, client resource allocation, and unit-economics rules.

Therefore, token measurement, value per token, marginal allocation, hierarchy, carry-forward, ROI, observability, or stage gates cannot be claimed as individual novelties.

## Surviving contribution under test

The inspected sources do not yet establish, as a single prospectively evaluated method, all of the following:

1. an episode-level reconciliation from AI trace to fully loaded cost;
2. a predefined outcome contract and measurement window;
3. independently reviewable outcome evidence;
4. an explicit counterfactual or comparison baseline;
5. attribution-confidence and uncertainty fields;
6. a signed or immutable decision receipt showing how the evidence caused a stop/revise/scale/allocation decision;
7. empirical measurement of false ROI, false scale, and false stop decisions against simpler accounting policies.

The paper's contribution should be this **outcome-evidence and decision-accountability bridge**. Hierarchical budgeting, reserve pooling, and token optimization remain application mechanisms and secondary experiments—not the novelty headline.

## Prohibited claims

Do not state that the study is the first enterprise AI ROI framework, the first AI tokenomics framework, the first value-per-token method, the first marginal allocation method, or the first hierarchical/carry-forward budget system. Do not equate consumption attribution with causal value attribution. Do not represent provider credits as transferable unless contract evidence establishes that fact.

## Evidence limitations

This audit is extensive scoping, not proof of global novelty. Several 2026 sources are recent preprints or working papers; two close SSRN items were assessed from their authoritative abstracts because full-text retrieval was blocked in the current environment. Patent review is a technical overlap screen, not a legal freedom-to-operate opinion. Database result counts were not always exposed. The search must be refreshed before submission, and the closest full texts must be independently checked when accessible.

## Gate consequence

**NARROW permits the next phase only under the revised title, question, and claim above.** The next artifacts are:

1. construct dictionary;
2. causal diagram separating usage attribution from incremental outcome attribution;
3. formal estimands for false ROI, false scale, false stop, verified value, and fully loaded cost;
4. a pilot protocol comparing evidence-ledger decisions with consumption-only and self-reported-value baselines.

No positive effectiveness claim is authorized.
