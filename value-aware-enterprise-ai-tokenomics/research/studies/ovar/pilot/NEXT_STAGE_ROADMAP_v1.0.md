# Next-Stage Roadmap v1.0

## Immediate human inputs

Two independent reviewers should complete the reviewer package. Recommended expertise is (A) finance/causal measurement and (B) operations/risk/audit. They must receive only reviewer-visible material and identical instructions.

## After reviews

1. Calculate exact agreement, weighted kappa for ordinal dimensions, and dimension-specific disagreement counts.
2. Create an adjudication register without silently averaging scores.
3. Revise unclear cases or rubrics once, recording every change and reason.
4. Create 48 new calibration cases across the same six domains, separate from the 24 engineering cases.
5. Predefine case-construction strata to include genuine positive, negative, neutral, indeterminate, compliance-blocked, delayed-value, shared-outcome, hidden-cost, weak-baseline, and low-adoption/high-value examples.
6. Conduct an independent leakage and construction-pattern review.
7. Freeze a prospective analysis plan and immutable package before running calibration.

## Later confirmatory path

Only after a prospective calibration gate passes should a held-out benchmark be constructed and sealed. A future field study should use randomized access, stepped rollout, or another defensible identification design and should report outcomes separately from usage telemetry.

## Manuscript status

Sections on the problem, prior work, construct definitions, method, integrity controls, and engineering dry run may be outlined. The abstract, comparative claims, and conclusion remain on hold until prospective evidence determines whether the paper is positive, negative, or methods-only.
