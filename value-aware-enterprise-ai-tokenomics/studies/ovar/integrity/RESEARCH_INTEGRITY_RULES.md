# Research Integrity Rules

1. Novelty is provisional until a documented formal audit is complete.
2. Cite close and conflicting work; do not hide prior art to strengthen positioning.
3. Do not copy prose, equations, diagrams, taxonomies, scenarios, code, or data from the RAER project or external sources.
4. RAER may inform process structure only: staged protocols, frozen gates, leakage separation, manifests, and transparent negative results.
5. Record every search query, source, access date, inclusion decision, quotation, and claim mapping.
6. Distinguish source facts, author interpretations, hypotheses, simulations, and empirical findings.
7. Do not use token counts as productivity, quality, or value labels.
8. Do not call gross benefit incremental value without an explicit baseline and attribution method.
9. Freeze hypotheses, outcomes, baselines, thresholds, allocation policies, and exclusions before confirmatory evaluation.
10. Keep design, validation, and held-out evidence separate; never tune on held-out outcomes.
11. Preserve negative and null results. Do not relax thresholds after seeing results.
12. Disclose material AI assistance according to venue policy; human authors remain responsible for every claim and citation.
13. Run an institution-approved similarity review before submission; never invent a similarity percentage.
14. A public repository search reduces duplication risk but cannot guarantee global uniqueness or freedom to operate.
