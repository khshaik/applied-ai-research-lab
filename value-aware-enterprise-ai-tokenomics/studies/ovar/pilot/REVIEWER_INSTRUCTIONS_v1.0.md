# OVAR Pilot Reviewer Instructions v1.0

## Blinding

Use only the reviewer workbook or `reviewer_visible_cases.json`. Do not request or inspect investigator labels, policy outputs, aggregate results, or another reviewer's scores.

## Scoring scale

Use integer scores from 1 to 5:

- **1 — not usable:** the construct cannot be judged or the information is materially contradictory;
- **2 — weak:** substantial missing detail would make decisions unreliable;
- **3 — adequate:** scoreable but important ambiguity remains;
- **4 — strong:** clear and decision-relevant with only minor limitations;
- **5 — exemplary:** unusually clear, auditable, and decision-ready for a constructed pilot.

## Dimensions

1. **Outcome-contract clarity:** specificity of outcome, population/workflow, measurement window, and acceptance boundary.
2. **Baseline credibility:** ability of the stated design to support an incremental comparison; score the design, not whether the result is favorable.
3. **Evidence auditability:** whether an independent reviewer could inspect the stated evidence status and reproduce the outcome assessment.
4. **Cost-boundary completeness:** visibility of provider, infrastructure, tooling, human review, integration, governance, and rework cost.
5. **Attribution defensibility:** whether the attribution confidence is plausible given the baseline and evidence.
6. **Decision realism:** whether the case provides a realistic stop/revise/continue/scale decision point.

For every 1 or 5, add a boundary rationale. Flag any phrase that appears to reveal an investigator label or preferred policy. Do not infer hidden results.
