## Required project controls

1. Maintain separate blinded-review and identified camera-ready manuscripts.
2. Do not cite a public repository in a manner that reveals author identity during double-blind review.
3. Fit the final paper within 12–15 Springer-formatted pages unless the 2026 instructions state otherwise.
4. Keep the abstract and contribution claims provisional until experimental results exist.
5. Prioritize one complete, defensible study over an oversized enterprise architecture description.
6. Include limitations, ethics, reproducibility, data/code availability, and material generative-AI disclosure as required by the venue or publisher.

## Submission viability assessment

OVAR is thematically suitable, but an architecture or opinion paper alone is unlikely to provide sufficient research weight. A submission should contain:

- a bounded novelty claim grounded in the formal audit;
- a formal, reproducible outcome-linked allocation method;
- a versioned ledger/schema;
- an empirical comparison with prespecified baselines;
- uncertainty and failure analysis;
- a prospective decision gate and integrity record.

Given the short interval to 25 August 2026, the feasible submission scope must be decided immediately after the novelty gate. Any result produced solely to meet the deadline must still satisfy the frozen protocol.
