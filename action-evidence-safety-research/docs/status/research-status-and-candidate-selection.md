# Research status and ThinkAI candidate selection

## Meaning of “complete”

Research completion must distinguish a properly closed registered study from validated effectiveness.

| Project | Registered work closed? | Effectiveness validated? | Accurate classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| OVAR (`value-aware-enterprise-ai-tokenomics`) | Yes, for OVAR v1 | No | Complete negative calibration study; broader research program remains incomplete |
| RAER (`action-evidence-safety-research`) | Yes, for RAER v1 and v2 | No | Complete methodological negative-result study |
| Story-points/VDCM (`storypoints-age-of-ai`) | No for the contemplated submission package | No | Research and evidence mapping remained in progress at the decision point |

## OVAR conclusion

The 24-case pilot and 48-case prospectively locked calibration were completed. OVAR failed four of nine mandatory gates, `OUTCOME_FLAT` dominated OVAR v1 across the registered burden range, and the formal decision was `STOP_OVAR_V1_NO_HELD_OUT`. No held-out benchmark, real organizational data, human validation, ROI field test, or superiority result exists.

Accurate wording: **completed methodological and negative-calibration research; empirical validation remains future work**. A future OVAR v2 requires a revised mechanism, new cases, a new preregistration, and new locks.

## RAER conclusion

RAER completed benchmark construction/review/adjudication, v1 validation, and the prospectively frozen v2 design evaluation. V1 stopped before held-out evaluation. V2 passed seven of eight criteria but achieved 25/27 = 92.6% safe completion, below the required 95%; the decision was `FAIL_KEEP_HELD_OUT_SEALED`.

Accurate wording: **the present RAER study is complete and publishable as a methodological negative-result study**. It is not validated effectiveness. Any successor effectiveness study is separate future research, not unfinished work in the closed study.

## ThinkAI ranking at the decision point

1. **RAER:** strongest candidate because it has the most complete evidence chain, prospective governance, adjudicated 96-case benchmark, executable tests, results, stopping rules, bootstrap intervals, ablations, closure records, and publication ledgers.
2. **OVAR:** viable negative-calibration paper, but weaker because it failed four of nine gates and did not reach held-out evaluation.
3. **Story-points/VDCM:** not ready for the deadline because evidence mapping and the working manuscript remained in progress.

Decision: submit RAER only for the 25 August deadline unless the conference explicitly permits multiple papers and every author/submission check can be completed independently.
