# Research Governance

## Evidence classes

- **Exploratory:** may guide method development but cannot support confirmatory claims.
- **Design:** exposed development or validation evidence used for selection or diagnosis.
- **Held-out:** inaccessible before an immutable release decision and a frozen evaluator.
- **Restricted:** investigator-only material that is not committed to Git.

## Immutability

Frozen records are append-only. Corrections receive a new version and a manifest that states what changed and why. A failed gate remains a failed gate; later fitted or exploratory results cannot replace it.

## Review provenance

Synthetic reviewers may stress-test clarity and rubric consistency, but their agreement is AI-AI consistency rather than human inter-rater reliability. Publications must preserve that distinction.

## Publication boundary

The RAER v2 design data are non-confirmatory. The held-out labels remain sealed. Repository availability does not establish external validity, production readiness, superiority, or global novelty.

## Double-blind submissions

Keep the repository private until the venue permits author identification. A public repository, Git history, license notice, or Zenodo record can reveal authorship even if the submitted PDF is anonymous.
