# ThinkAI 2026 camera-ready checklist

Complete this only after acceptance and after receiving the final organizer instructions.

## Reviewer revision

- [ ] Apply and verify every item in [`KNOWN_UPDATE_GAP.md`](KNOWN_UPDATE_GAP.md).
- [ ] Record every reviewer comment and the corresponding response/change.
- [ ] Preserve the negative-result boundary; do not imply held-out validation or deployment effectiveness.
- [ ] Re-run every affected calculation and test after substantive changes.
- [ ] Update the claim-to-evidence ledger if wording or evidence locators change.
- [ ] Reverify every citation and DOI.

## Identity and declarations

- [ ] Confirm final author names and order.
- [ ] Confirm affiliations and corresponding-author email.
- [ ] Add ORCID identifiers where available.
- [ ] Confirm funding statement.
- [ ] Confirm competing-interest statement.
- [ ] Confirm ethics/consent statement for the constructed non-human-participant study.
- [ ] Confirm CRediT statement.
- [ ] Confirm data/code availability statement and permanent repository/DOI.
- [ ] Retain an accurate generative-AI disclosure; human authors remain accountable.
- [ ] Add acknowledgements that were omitted or generalized for anonymous review.

## Production package

- [ ] Use the organizer-confirmed Springer source format and template.
- [ ] Supply DOCX plus linked figures, or LaTeX source, bibliography, and figures, as requested.
- [ ] Produce and inspect the final PDF page by page.
- [ ] Check tables, equations, references, figure labels, resolution, page count, and metadata.
- [ ] Complete similarity and author proof checks.
- [ ] Complete the Springer license-to-publish or copyright paperwork.
- [ ] Pay registration by the stated deadline.
- [ ] Update CMT metadata so it exactly matches the final manuscript.

## Artifact release

- [ ] Release only reviewer-visible benchmark data and exposed design labels permitted by policy.
- [ ] Include code, tests, protocols, raw design outputs, bootstrap data, ablations, locks, closure manifests, citation log, and claim ledger if supplements are permitted.
- [ ] Exclude the investigator vault, coordinator workbook, access log, and sealed held-out labels.
- [ ] Keep the repository private until venue policy permits deanonymization.
