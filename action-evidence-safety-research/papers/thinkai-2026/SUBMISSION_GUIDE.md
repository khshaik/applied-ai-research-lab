## Source hierarchy and caveat

The live [ThinkAI paper-submission page](https://thinkai.klh.edu.in/paper-submission) currently identifies itself as ThinkAI 2025 and exposes only the Microsoft CMT disclosure in accessible page content. It does not conclusively state the full 2026 page, anonymity, or source-package rules.

The historical [official ThinkAI 2024 submission page](https://klh.edu.in/ThinkAI2024/papersubmission.html) required 12-15 pages, LNCS/CCIS one-column format, approximately 400 words per page, and double-blind review. This is useful precedent, not a substitute for 2026 confirmation.

Springer maintains current [Computer Science proceedings templates and author instructions](https://link.springer.com/series/558/information-for-authors-and-editors) for LNCS, LNAI, LNBI, LNBIP, CCIS, LNICST, and IFIP AICT.

## August review submission

Submit a complete anonymous review paper, not an identified camera-ready file. The manuscript must include the complete problem, contribution, related work, novelty boundary, hypotheses, benchmark and design, all results including the failed gate, limitations, threats to validity, conclusion, and references.

Use the PDF in [`manuscript/initial-submission/`](manuscript/initial-submission/) after completing the folder checklist. Word is the lowest-risk editable source because the verified manuscript is already in DOCX. A late conversion to LaTeX is unnecessary unless the 2026 organizers require it.

## December camera-ready submission

Only if accepted, start from [`manuscript/camera-ready-submission/`](manuscript/camera-ready-submission/), address reviewer comments, restore and verify author identity, complete declarations and license paperwork, create the final source/PDF package, and perform a new page-by-page QA pass.

## arXiv guidance

arXiv is a preprint repository, not the conference manuscript format.

- Conference format: organizer-confirmed Springer proceedings format.
- Suggested arXiv primary category: `cs.AI`.
- Reasonable secondary category: `cs.SE`.
- Do not use `cs.LG` unless the final paper makes a substantive machine-learning contribution.
- Label the upload as a preprint and later add the formal Springer citation/DOI.
- During likely double-blind review, defer an identified arXiv upload unless the organizers confirm in writing that preprints are permitted and will not compromise anonymity.

## AI-use guidance

Springer Nature's [AI guidance](https://group.springernature.com/gp/group/ai/ai-guidance-for-our-researchers-and-communities) requires human accountability, prohibits listing AI tools as authors, requires transparent declaration of material AI use, and generally disallows generative-AI images except limited documented cases. RAER uses data/code-generated tables and no generative-AI image; preserve the underlying scripts and data.
