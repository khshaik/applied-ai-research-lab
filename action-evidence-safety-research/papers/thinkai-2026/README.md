## Decision in one sentence

Submit the anonymous RAER paper for the 25 August 2026 full-paper deadline; retain the identified camera-ready package for revision only if the paper is accepted.

## Scientific boundary

RAER v2 is a completed **methodological negative-result study**, not a validated-effectiveness study. It passed seven of eight prospective design criteria but achieved 25/27 = 92.6% safe completion against the required 95%. The formal decision is `FAIL_KEEP_HELD_OUT_SEALED`; the 24-case held-out partition was not released or accessed.

Keep this repository private during double-blind review. Share only the anonymous PDF through the confirmed submission portal unless the organizers explicitly authorize anonymous supplementary material.
